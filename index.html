Tip Math

Four people, one check, and a penny nobody can split.

Boundary: arithmetic only You choose the tip percent and whether it goes on the tax. Tip Math never says what is polite or what you should leave - it only does the cents.

The tip truth

20% of $48.60 is $9.72 - the check comes to $58.32. Whole cents, rounded once.

The tax truth

Tip on $100.00 is $20.00; on $108.00 with tax it is $21.60 - tipping on the tax costs $1.60 more. Your call, now with the price on it.

The split truth

$127.43 across 4 is $31.85 each with 3 cents left over - 3 people pay $31.86 and 1 pays $31.85. The leftover cents have an owner.
